   > *"My cardiologist started me on Concor 5mg OD morning, Lipiget 20mg 1 tab at night after dinner, and Lowplat 75mg once daily after lunch. Can you schedule these?"*

3. **Complex Diabetic Regimen:**
   > *"Doctor prescribed Glucophage 500mg twice daily with meals (BD), Januvia 100mg once daily in the morning (OD), and Diamicron MR 60mg before breakfast. Please organize my daily dose slots."*

   > *"Prescription: Azithromycin (Azomax) 500mg OD for 3 days, Hydryllin syrup 2 teaspoons TDS for 5 days, and Paracetamol 500mg SOS for fever > 100°F."*

5. **Steroid Tapering Course:**

6. **Gastroenterology Regimen with Meal Timings:**

7. **Psychiatric Medication Titration:**

## 💡 Quick Tips for Testing
- **Test Speech Dictation**: Click **`🎙️ Speak`** on any prompt to test hands-free dictation in English or Urdu.
- **Test OCR Image Attachments**: Click **`📷 Attach`** with any prescription image to verify automatic table extraction and schedule populating.
- **Test Audio Read-Aloud**: Click **`🔊 Listen`** on the assistant's reply to hear synthesized speech feedback.
- **Test Timetable Add**: On prescriptions, click **`[➕ Add all to Medication Cabinet & Timetable]`** and verify dose rows immediately appear on the `/schedule` page.
